CompGod 1.0.0 - Windows x64 VST3 effect

1. Close the DAW.
2. Copy the complete CompGod.vst3 folder to:
   C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3
   (administrator permission may be needed)
   Or use a VST3 location your DAW is configured to scan.
3. Rescan plugins in the DAW.
4. Insert CompGod on an audio track, choose a preset, and adjust Threshold.
5. Match loudness with Makeup. Use Bypass to compare.

Keep the Contents subfolder inside CompGod.vst3 intact.

CompGod.exe is the optional standalone version. Use its audio settings to
choose an input and output. A DAW with the VST3 is recommended for tracks.

Source, manual OCR text, controls, build instructions and validation notes
are included. The model is inspired by the hardware manual, not measured
against the physical hardware. There is no output limiter.
